## The merged foundations

[PR #4727](https://github.com/kvcache-ai/Mooncake/pull/4727) merged a bounded backend. [PR #5310](https://github.com/kvcache-ai/Mooncake/pull/5310) merged ordered recovery records for physical upload and cleanup obligations. The logical key remains under Master metadata.

## The integration proposal

[PR #5494](https://github.com/kvcache-ai/Mooncake/pull/5494) is open. Its synchronous design durably authorizes one immutable object attempt, uploads it, then validates the attempt at commit. Publication follows the durable log acknowledgment. Read descriptors protected by a lease let another client fetch bytes directly from storage.

## Release the right physical object

Removing or replacing a logical value retires the corresponding physical object. A reader can retain its earlier lease. If an upload is still uncertain, a late PUT can materialize after a successful DELETE, so cleanup must preserve that obligation. See the [synchronous design RFC](https://github.com/kvcache-ai/Mooncake/issues/5495).
